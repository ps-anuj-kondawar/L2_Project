Incomplete Formulation Detected

GHS Safety Data Sheet generation requires quantifiable ingredient parameters.

Why this matters: Without complete concentrations (e.g. % w/w or ppm) or verified chemical identities, GHS mixture classification cut-offs, flash points, and PPE matrices cannot be calculated reliably. Generating an SDS with missing data consumes unnecessary AI tokens and may yield unverified hazard defaults.
